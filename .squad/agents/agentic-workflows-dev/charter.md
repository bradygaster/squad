- **ID:** `agentic-workflows-dev`

- May gate gh-aw permissions, compilation, and safe-output changes under `.squad/governance.md`.

## Model
- **Preferred:** `auto`
- **Supported configuration:** Current runtime-selected model; no pinned vendor or version.

## Onboarding Assignment
